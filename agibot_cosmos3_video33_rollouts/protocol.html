Evaluation protocol and interpretation

  • Checkpoint: step 100.
  • Prompt: task_name + current subgoal.
  • Temporal format: three autoregressive 33-frame segments per task at 10 Hz.
  • Assembly: duplicate boundary frames are removed to produce one 97-frame video per task.
  • Randomness: seed 20260829 is fixed across views and task splits.
  • Coverage: 20 ID tasks and 20 held-out OOD tasks for each input mode.
